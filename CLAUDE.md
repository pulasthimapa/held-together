# Held Together: working instructions

Channel: true stories of the people who built the world, told by a recurring illustrated cast in Indeepa's cloned voice. The evidence behind these rules is in `docs/research-knowledge.md`.

## Stories and scripts
1. Only true stories. Every factual claim needs an opened source recorded in the episode's fact sheet. Never use a search snippet or AI summary as a source. When sources disagree, use the safer wording and note the disagreement in the description.
2. Emotion comes from real people and real documents (letters, telegrams, testimony), paraphrased and credited. No invented suffering, no graphic death imagery, no shock framing in titles.
3. No two episodes share a story skeleton. Vary the opening (the event, a document, the aftermath, an object), the structure and the ending. Only the cast faces stay fixed.
4. Every episode has an Engineer's note written and recorded by Indeepa in his **real voice**, not the clone. It is the channel's main human signal.
5. Write the title and thumbnail idea before the script. Drop a story that has no clear thumbnail moment.

5a. After each upload, count comments that mention AI, robots or fake voices. If they appear on two episodes in a row, propose switching the main narration to Indeepa's real voice. Viewers in story niches reject AI narration (research finding 11).
5b. Lock the story structure (beats and ending) before generating any images.

## Cast and visuals
6. The troupe never plays members of a specific Indigenous or cultural community in costume. Those people appear through archival photos or respectful symbols.
7. Every shot moves, and engineering diagrams are drawn in code. No still-image slideshows.

## Output and publishing
8. One long episode a week, plus vertical cuts. Never mass-upload.
9. Vertical cuts must be at least 61 seconds, so they qualify for TikTok rewards. Upload them natively to each platform, never with another platform's watermark.
10. Every YouTube upload ticks "Altered or synthetic content" and lists sources in the description.
11. Once a month, post one human behind-the-scenes item: a sketch, a source document, or Indeepa talking through a calculation.

## Working rules for Claude
12. Separate facts, hypotheses and conclusions. Label single-source claims as unverified.
13. Before stating any platform rule or price, re-check it, as they change often. Re-check YouTube monetisation rules every quarter.
14. Keep monthly tool spend under £30 until revenue starts. Say so before any change that raises it.
